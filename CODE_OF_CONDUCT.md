# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenInn to be a welcoming project for everyone: hosts, developers and first-time contributors alike. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project's users
- showing empathy and kindness towards others

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults or derogatory comments
- personal or political attacks
- publishing others' private information without permission, including guest data from your PMS
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/TMSifat). All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
