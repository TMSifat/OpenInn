# Seaside Cottage — guest guide

## Wi-Fi
- Network: SeasideCottage
- Password: shells-and-sand-42

## Check-in and check-out
- Check-in from 4:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM.
- Self check-in with the keypad on the front door. The code is the last 4 digits of the guest's phone number.
- Early check-in or late check-out is only possible if the host approves it. Always ask the host.
- Luggage can be left in the porch storage box from 12:00 PM on arrival day.

## Parking
- One free parking space in the driveway (the left side).
- Free street parking on Harbour Road after 6:00 PM.

## House rules
- No smoking anywhere, including the garden.
- No parties or events. Quiet hours 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.
- Dogs are welcome (max 1). No other pets.
- Maximum 4 guests.

## The house
- 2 bedrooms (1 queen, 2 singles), 1 bathroom.
- Washing machine under the kitchen counter. Detergent is in the cupboard above it.
- Beach towels are in the hallway closet. Please don't take bath towels to the beach.
- Heating: thermostat in the hallway. Set it between 18 and 22 °C.
- Rubbish: black bin for general waste, blue bin for recycling. Collection is Tuesday morning.

## Local tips
- Beach: 3 minutes' walk, down the path at the end of the road.
- Coffee: Driftwood Café on Harbour Road, opens 7:30 AM.
- Dinner: The Anchor pub (great fish and chips), 10 minutes' walk.
- Groceries: Co-op on Station Street, open until 10:00 PM.

## Emergencies
- For anything urgent, the assistant should hand the conversation to the host.
